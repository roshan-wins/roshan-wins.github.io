UAV wing optimization

Parametric external-aerodynamics CFD on UAV wing geometries, with NSGA-II multi-objective optimization to pick the aerodynamic attachments that balance drag against efficiency.

Role
CFD engineer intern
Where
Reude Technologies
When
January–May 2024
Tools
CFD, NSGA-II

The problem

A UAV wing has to carry the aircraft with as little drag as possible, and attachments on the wing shift that balance. The question was which attachment configuration gives the best trade-off between drag and aerodynamic efficiency.

Approach

  • Parametric external-aerodynamics CFD on the wing geometries, so each design in the space could be generated and simulated the same way.
  • NSGA-II, a genetic algorithm for several objectives, searched the attachment design space for designs on the Pareto front between drag and efficiency, and the final configuration was picked from that front.

Result

The final configuration was chosen from the Pareto front. Its geometry and numbers are confidential, so the chart above shows the method, not the data.
