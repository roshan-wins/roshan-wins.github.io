The brief

An indexing jig with a base plate, a body, a clamping ring and a spring-loaded plunger that locks each position, modelled in SolidWorks and released as an A2 assembly drawing. A pair assignment in a KTH CAD course.

A spring driven by numbers

The compression spring is a helix sweep driven by global variables: wire diameter, mean coil diameter, free length and number of coils. That made it quick to resize from the gaps measured in the assembly, 20 mm with the plunger locked and 12 mm unlocked. The first coil count made the spring go solid at 12 mm, so the final spring uses a 1.0 mm wire with 8 coils.

Configurations that move the mechanism

The spring has a locked and an unlocked configuration, linked to matching configurations of the assembly. Switching the assembly configuration moves the plunger, so the drawing and any interference check can show both states.

Releasing the lock

A lever on a pivot through the M12 hole pulls the plunger back against the spring. The A2 drawing shows orthographic, section and exploded views, overall dimensions, a parts list with balloons and the KTH title block.
