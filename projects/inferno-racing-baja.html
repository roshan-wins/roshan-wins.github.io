The team

Inferno Racing builds an all-terrain car for the SAE Baja student competition. I was team captain and lead design engineer from 2021 to 2024, leading 25 student engineers through the full design, build and test cycle. We placed 3rd nationwide.

What I designed

  • The transmission and the suspension systems.
  • The chassis check: finite element analysis of the frame against static and dynamic load cases, to a minimum factor of safety of 1.5.

Building it

I led manufacturing from raw stock to final assembly. That meant specifying heat treatment for the hardened gears and shafts and solving process problems on the shop floor as they came up.

At the event

The competition meant taking the team and the car across the country. I was responsible for both through the whole event, and when the gearbox failed, I made the fixes on the spot and kept us in it.
