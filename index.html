About

I want to be the designer on the team, and a generalist across the whole vehicle. I have loved cars since I was a child: Japanese passion, German engineering, Ferrari and Koenigsegg.

I was born in Chennai, and I love the city. I studied mechanical engineering there, at Anna University, and captained our SAE Baja team, Inferno Racing. At Morphle Labs I took imaging machines from CAD to the field as R&D engineer and product owner. Since 2025 I have been studying for an MSc in Vehicle Engineering at KTH in Stockholm, where I also designed suspension parts for the RCV-Dynamic research vehicle.
