EV battery cold plate

CFD in Ansys Fluent to even out the coolant flow across an electric-vehicle battery cold plate and cut its pressure drop, with scripted geometry so a design of experiments could sweep the shapes.

The problem

A battery cold plate only works if coolant reaches every part of it. If the flow favours some paths through the plate and starves others, the cells over the starved regions run hotter, and the pump works against a pressure drop that buys nothing. The job was to even out the coolant distribution and bring the pressure drop down.

Approach

  • Built the plate geometry parametrically in Ansys SpaceClaim and automated its generation, so a design of experiments could sweep shapes without remodelling by hand.
  • Ran the sweeps in Ansys Fluent and fitted response surfaces to the results to find the combination that balanced uniformity against pressure drop.

Result

The optimized plate reached a flow uniformity index of 97 % with a lower pressure drop, and the design is now used in buses.
